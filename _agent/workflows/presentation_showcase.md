# 🎭 Financial Integrity Showcase Workflow

Follow these steps to demonstrate the absolute reliability and strategic depth of AccountingFlow during the presentation.

## 1. 📂 Step 1: Genesis (Data Generation)
1. Go to **Dashboard**.
2. Click **[SYSTEM INTEGRITY TEST]** (bottom of the page).
3. Confirm when the alert says "『이건 데이터가 아니라 역사다.』".
4. **Why?** This seeds 3 years of complex accounting history (2026-2028) that proves the system can handle multi-year scale without a single won of discrepancy.

## 2. 🛡️ Step 2: The Truth (Financial Statements)
1. Navigate to **Financial Statements**.
2. Observe the **"BALANCED"** badge at the top.
3. Switch to **C/F (Cash Flow)** tab.
4. Show the **"Verified"** badge at the bottom.
5. **Key Narrative:** "Our Cash Flow statement doesn't just calculate numbers; it reconciles with the Balance Sheet's real cash in real-time. If there was even a 1-won 'ghost liability', this badge would turn red."

## 3. 🔍 Step 3: Drill-down & Audit Trail
1. In the **C/F tab**, click on **"영업자산/부채의 변동 (Working Capital)"**.
2. Show the **Component Breakdown** at the top of the modal.
3. Scroll through the transactions.
4. **Key Narrative:** "Every single won in our summary statements is traceable to a specific journal entry. We move from the CFO's high-level view to the auditor's micro-view in one click."

## 4. 🔦 Step 4: The Lie Detector (Sealing Engine)
1. Click **EXCEL** to export the current report.
2. Open the excel file (or just pretend to) and mention the hidden **Integrity Metadata** sheet.
3. Click the **VERIFY** button on the top right of the page.
4. Upload the file you just exported.
5. Observe the **"VERIFIED"** green banner.
6. **Key Narrative:** "This is our Sealing Engine. It creates a digital fingerprint of every exported report. Even if a user manually changes a 0 to a 5 in Excel, our system can detect the tampering instantly."

## 5. 🤖 Step 5: Strategic AI Forecast
1. Go back to **Dashboard**.
2. Scroll to the **AI Cash Flow Prediction** panel.
3. Toggle between **REALITY** and **ROSE-COLORED** modes.
4. **Key Narrative:** "Our AI doesn't hallucinate. It uses the same verified ledger data to simulate two futures: the cold reality and the strategic goal. This is the difference between an accounting tool and a CFO's cockpit."
